# Switchyard Sprint gameplay rules

Simulation owns all movement and scoring; React reads snapshots. Coordinates are metres, feet Y-up, forward +Z, lanes X=-3, 0, 3. Actors have a 0.32m horizontal collision radius and 1.65m standing / 0.60m sliding height. A tick is 1/60s. FixedStep caps catch-up at eight ticks and discards excess wall time, so a long background gap does not fast-forward a run. Focus loss and page hiding pause. Resume always requires a user action and a three-second countdown with frozen world/effect timers.

Speed starts at 12m/s, gains 0.075m/s each second, and caps at 26m/s. Tutorial remains at 12m/s. Lane movement travels at 22m/s; each distinct press changes exactly one target lane. Key repeat is ignored. Airborne lane changes are allowed. Jump starts at 10m/s under 25m/s² gravity. Down while airborne immediately changes vertical speed to at most -12m/s; grounded down slides for 0.85s. The Tide board adds 9% jump impulse; Ember reduces falling gravity and limits descent to 5m/s until the player deliberately presses down.

Train roofs are 2.7m high. Ramps rise over 12m and connect flush to 14m train bodies. Feet follow slopes and fall after roof edges. Ramp sides block entry below the surface. Collision uses relative-motion swept AABBs for solid hazards, with a wedge check for ramp sides; the head and feet reflect jumps, slides and elevated surfaces. Bounded moving trains shunt along a ±5m section with a 0.65 rad/s phase. Their continuous position and speed are included in collision checks.

A side entry into a hazard causes a stumble, pushes the player back to the previous lane, and moves the pursuing drone close. A frontal impact defeats immediately. A second impact while the drone is close is fatal. Side recovery gives 1.1s invulnerability. The drone gradually falls back during clean running. On defeat the drone approaches the player while the run stays frozen for a revive or results choice.

Both boards last 18s and absorb one crash. Shield consumption removes nearby hazards ahead, grants 2.3s protection, and destroys the board. Natural board expiry gives 0.75s protection. Board activation is disabled during jetpack flight; a board already running continues to count down during flight. Charges are deducted by the shell once on the board activation event.

Power-ups activate on contact. Base durations are magnet 10s, jetpack 8s, super-jump shoes 10s and score multiplier 12s. Each purchased duration level adds 3s, with three levels. Different effects coexist. Repeating the same pickup refreshes that effect to its full upgraded duration; durations never add indefinitely. Pause/countdown/caught/results freeze all durations. Magnet attracts ground and roof coins across all three lanes within 10m, but does not vacuum the jetpack's high coin trail from the ground. Jetpack raises feet to 6.5m, avoids collision, and has coin lines at 7.2m. Flight expiry clears a landing corridor and gives 2.5s safe descent. Shoes multiply jump impulse by 1.42; expiry preserves the existing airborne trajectory and gives a short protected landing window. The temporary multiplier doubles both distance and coin score. Distance earns one score per metre times permanent multiplier; each coin adds ten points times active multipliers.

Head-start is an optional earned consumable: eight seconds of protected flight, magnet, and a 9m/s speed boost bounded by the speed cap. Revive price is handled by progression (one token, then two, then three for every later revive); simulation accepts a revive only from caught, increments its count, removes hazards for 80m, resets the player to track level in the selected lane, resets drone distance, and starts a three-second countdown with three seconds of subsequent protection. Currency is committed outside simulation through idempotent progression transactions.

The generator streams 200m chunks at least 420m ahead and retires behind the player. Four obstacle rows at offsets 34, 78, 122 and 166 each reserve a clear lane; coins indicate that route. Generation leaves gaps between expanded moving-train and ramp envelopes so a maximum-speed actor can change two lanes. The certificate changes target lane 30m before each row. Optional routes offer hurdles, slides and train roofs; no power-up is required. Districts change every 600m through Sunline Station, Painted Quarter and Copper Quay. Another city's district descriptions and assets can replace the catalog without changing the track simulation.

Tutorial asks for left, right, jump, down, board activation and twelve coins. Collisions recover freely. Completing the six tasks and passing 170m produces results. Main runs are endless. Local challenges use fixed seeds: Sunline Dash (17329 / 750m), Market Money (89271 / 75 coins), Quay High (424242 / 10,000 score). Completing an objective marks the run, while play continues to improve its local record. Developer preview can activate effects directly, and its summary mode is `preview` so normal rewards and records stay isolated.

Automated verification in `tests/simulation.test.ts` covers 1,000 seeds across four chunks using actual swept actor movement at 12/19/26m/s, moving-obstacle phases and elevated entry heights, optional roof traversal, input timing, hazards, shield and revive behavior, each effect's pickup/repeat/pause/expiry, safe jetpack landing, the complete tutorial, 30/60/144Hz consistency, bounded long stalls, and 20 minutes of simulated streaming. This synthetic long run checks object bounds; real render performance and manual game feel are separately reported by the project performance record.
